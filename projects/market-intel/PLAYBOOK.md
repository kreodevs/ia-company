# Market Intel — operator playbook

1. **Apply vertical pack** from Products → Vertical packs (registers product + workflows).
2. **Commission first scan** — Office recepción or Estrategia → *Market scan* service.
3. **Review deliverables** in `/office/archive` filtered by product.
4. **Deliver to client** — encargo detail → Share with client (delivery link).
5. **Record revenue** — encargo detail → Revenue panel when invoice is paid.

## Repeat engagements

| Client need | Service | Department |
|-------------|---------|------------|
| Broad scan | `market-scan` | Estrategia |
| Deep dive | `research-drilldown` preset | Estrategia |
| GO/NO-GO | `idea-validation` | Estrategia |
| Pricing report | `pricing-review` | Negocio |

## Exit criteria

- ≥1 delivered encargo with client delivery link
- ≥1 revenue event linked to that encargo
